2Retrieval — the variable RQ1 Same generator, prompt, chunks & top-3 throughout — only the retriever changes, isolating its effect.
Baseline

BM25

Sparse lexical match (keyword overlap) over the full 84-filing corpus. No semantics.

Pipeline 2

Dense · FAISS

Cosine search over MiniLM embeddings. Captures meaning, bridges vocabulary gaps.

Pipeline 3

Enhanced

An LLM rewrites the question into a search query, then dense retrieval runs on it.
